Responsible AI Principles

✓ Transparency

A connected system should identify the documents and sources supporting its responses.

✓ No fabricated information

The assistant must not invent claim status, eligibility, deadlines, weather information or official decisions.

✓ Human oversight

Important, unclear or disputed decisions must be reviewed by authorised people.

✓ Privacy and consent

Personal information should only be collected and used for a clearly explained purpose.

✓ Fairness and accessibility

The design aims to support different languages, literacy levels and ways of communicating.

✓ Uncertainty disclosure

Missing, outdated or conflicting information must be disclosed instead of being presented as fact.
